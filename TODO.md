- [ ] **Touch scroll does nothing inside tmux on the Pocket** (added 2026-10-09, Joshua). Touchscreen swipe scrolls a plain Ghostty window but not a tmux pane; prefix + `[` then up-motions still scroll, and the wheel path is on (`set -g mouse on`). **Cause found 2026-10-09:** a test window that turned on mouse reporting (as tmux does) logged every touch as `ESC[<0;1;1M` / `ESC[<0;1;1m` — a left click at cell 1,1 — with no motion and no wheel events. Ghostty 1.3.1 does its own touch scroll only when no app asks for mouse input, and has no touch config options. Not fixable in tmux or Ghostty config (mouse off would also kill wheel scroll in tmux). Options: report upstream to Ghostty (touch while mouse reporting is on should send wheel/motion at the real position), or a Hyprland touch-gesture plugin (hyprgrass) mapping a swipe to scroll. Until then: prefix + `[`.
